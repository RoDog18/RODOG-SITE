Features

See every hit

A scrolling waveform shows the input, what passes and every trigger. Drag the open and close lines right on it.

Lookahead and hysteresis

Opens before the hit so the attack is never clipped, and never chatters on a decaying tom.

Bleed reject

Route the snare mic to the side chain and its hits are ignored by the kick gate.

Listen

Hear the output, the key, or exactly what's removed.
