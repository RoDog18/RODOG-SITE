Dog House SuiteDynamic EQ

DH-BloodHound-EQ

A surgical 64-band dynamic EQ that shows you where your tracks mask each other.

Price coming soon

Installs through the The Kennel. VST3 & AU, macOS & Windows.

▶DH-BloodHound-EQ walkthrough video coming soon

Features

64 bands, 9 shapes

Bell, shelves, cuts up to 96 dB/oct, notch, band pass and tilts, with Q from 0.025 to 1000, in stereo, left, right, mid or side.

Dynamic per band

Every band can compress or expand, cut or boost, listening to itself, the side chain or another track.

Three phase modes

ZERO LAT, NATURAL (analog-shaped curves with no latency) and LINEAR phase.

Masking and FETCH

Every Bloodhound shows which other tracks mask it and where. FETCH adds the cuts for you, live or as a snapshot, with priorities so the right track gives way.

Auto gain

Keeps the level the same as you EQ, so you hear the change, not the volume.
