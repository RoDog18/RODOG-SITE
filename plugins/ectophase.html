Ectophase

A phaser with eight spirits, from a gentle 4-stage wisp to a 24-stage legion.

Price coming soon

Installs through the RoDog Product Manager. VST3 & AU, macOS & Windows.

▶Ectophase walkthrough video coming soon

Features

Eight spirits

WISP (4 stages), SHADE (6), POLTERGEIST (8, thrown around at random), BANSHEE (12, screaming feedback), WRAITH (an endless rising sweep), POSSESSED (follows your playing), HAUNT (spins around the room) and LEGION (24 stages).

Full control

DEPTH, CENTRE, FEEDBACK in both directions and SPREAD, five LFO shapes and a free or synced RATE.

Possess, flip and still

POSSESS lets your playing drive it, FLIP inverts it and STILL freezes the sweep.

Eight advanced cards

DRIVE, TONE, DUCK, WIDTH, WOBBLE, STEP SEQ, SIDECHAIN and CURSE.
