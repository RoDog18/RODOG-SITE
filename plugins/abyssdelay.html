EVIL seriesDelay

EVIL-Abyssdelay-Delay

Seven delay modes, a rhythm pad that writes patterns, prime-number taps and MatchMe on the repeats.

PriceSet at launch

Installs through The Kennel. VST3 and AU, for macOS and Windows.

Walkthrough video coming soon

Features

Seven modes

SLAP, ECHO, PING-PONG, DIRTY, WRAITH (reverse), ABYSS (pitch falls with every repeat) and POSSESSED (a random event every repeat).

PATTERN

A rhythmic delay that writes itself. Drag across the pad for sparse to busy and up for more repeats.

PRIME

Six repeats at prime-number times that never line up, for a natural, scattered space.

Advanced

Tape wobble, filter sweep, diffusion, multi-tap rhythm, runaway SUMMON feedback, ducking and FREEZE.

MatchMe on the repeats

Match the repeats to the dry signal, the side chain or a file.
