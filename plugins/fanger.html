Fanger

Eight flavours of flange, from classic jet sweeps to through-zero tape and an endless spiral.

Features

Eight modes

JET is the classic jet sweep, ZERO HOUR is through-zero tape flanging, BLADE screams with short delays and metallic feedback, FLAY is hollow negative polarity, SWARM stacks three flangers, NOSEDIVE dive-bombs, HOWL follows how hard you play and SPIRAL rises forever.

▶ Video coming soon

Hands-on sweep

DEPTH, MANUAL (0.1 to 10 ms), FEEDBACK in both directions and SPREAD between left and right, with five LFO shapes and a free or tempo-synced RATE.

▶ Video coming soon

Through-zero, envelope and still

THRU-ZERO for real tape flanging, ENVELOPE to let your playing drive the sweep, and STILL to freeze it in place.

▶ Video coming soon

Eight advanced cards

BURN, TONE, DUCK, WIDTH, STEPS, SIDECHAIN, TRIGGER and MANGLE take it further when you need them.

▶ Video coming soon

A display that moves

Bats swoop across a blood moon with the sweep, and more of them come out the harder you push it.
