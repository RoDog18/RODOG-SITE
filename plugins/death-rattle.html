EVIL seriesTremolo

EVIL-DeathRattle-Tremolo

A tremolo with eight afflictions, from amp shiver and hard chop to harmonic trem and auto-pan.

PriceSet at launch

Installs through The Kennel. VST3 and AU, for macOS and Windows.

Walkthrough video coming soon

Features

Eight afflictions

SHIVER (classic amp tremolo), CHATTER (hard square chop), SPLIT TONGUE (harmonic tremolo), RATTLE (bursts that speed up), CONVULSE (random fits), PULSE (a heartbeat), SERPENT (auto-pan) and STRIKE (every hit restarts the cycle).

Seven shapes, synced or free

SINE, TRI, SQR, SAW, RAMP, S&H and HEART, with RATE from 1/64 to 4 bars, dotted and triplets, and RETRIG to lock it to the bar.

Shape the pulse

DEPTH, SMOOTH, SPREAD and BIAS set how deep, how soft and how wide the tremolo is.

Eight advanced cards

FEVER, TONE, DUCK, WIDTH, STEPS, SIDECHAIN, PALSY and VENOM.

A display that moves

The snake rattles, its eye glows and lightning cracks as the tremolo moves.
