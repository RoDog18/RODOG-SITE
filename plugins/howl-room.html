Three wooden rooms

SMALL is tight, MEDIUM sits in between, LARGE is big and open with more low end. DECAY, PRE-DELAY, TONE and WIDTH, with tempo sync.

Tail section

RELEASE, GRIT, PRESENCE and GRIT MIX only touch the reverb tail.

Match EQ inside

From MatchMe: learn a reference and the room's tail is EQ'd to match it, once or live.
