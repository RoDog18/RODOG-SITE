HOWL-Room-Reverb

A clean wooden room reverb with a built-in match EQ. The dog howls louder the more you add.

PriceSet at launch

Installs through The Kennel. VST3 and AU, for macOS and Windows.

Walkthrough video coming soon

Features

Three wooden rooms

SMALL is tight, MEDIUM sits in between, LARGE is big and open with more low end. DECAY, PRE-DELAY, TONE and WIDTH, with tempo sync.

Tail section

RELEASE, GRIT, PRESENCE and GRIT MIX only touch the reverb tail.

Match EQ inside

From MatchMe: learn a reference and the room's tail is EQ'd to match it, once or live.

It howls

The more reverb you dial in, the more the plugin turns into a wooden room and the louder the dog howls.
