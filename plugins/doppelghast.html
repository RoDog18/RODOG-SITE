EVIL seriesChorus

Doppelghast

A chorus with eight modes, from a classic stereo chorus to an octave-down shadow choir.

Price coming soon

Installs through the RoDog Product Manager. VST3 & AU, macOS & Windows.

▶Doppelghast walkthrough video coming soon

Features

Eight modes

SÉANCE (classic stereo chorus), COVEN (tri-chorus), HEX (fast warble), HORDE (big ensemble with shimmer), WRAITH (subtle mono-safe widener), DROWNED (slow and seasick), POSSESSED (follows your playing) and BLACK MASS (an octave-down choir).

Up to eight voices

DEPTH, DELAY (1 to 40 ms), VOICES (1 to 8), FEEDBACK both ways and SPREAD, with four LFO shapes and a free or synced RATE.

Vibrato and still

Switch to pure VIBRATO, or STILL to hold the modulation.

Eight advanced cards

BBD, TONE, DUCK, WIDTH, SHADOW, WHISPERS, SIDECHAIN and CURSE.
