EVIL seriesSynth

EVIL-Necrosynth-Synth

A synth with five sound engines, one-key chords, a rhythm pad and an arpeggiator.

PriceSet at launch

Installs through The Kennel. VST3 and AU, for macOS and Windows.

Walkthrough video coming soon

Features

Five modes

GRAVE (seven detuned saws and a sub), HEX (growl wavetables you warp), BANSHEE (hard sync and FM), CRYPT (a choir pad) and BONE (four-operator FM bells). Each has its own CHARACTER knob.

Devil chords

Play a whole chord from one key: minor, diminished, tritone, phrygian or power.

Ritual rhythm pad

Drag across for sparse to busy and up for depth. It gates, filters, pitches or warps the sound in time with the song, and the steps can be drawn by hand.

Arpeggiator

ARP plays held notes in the same rhythm: UP, DOWN, RANDOM or CURSED.

Effects built in

SCREAM, WHISPERS, CURSE, SUMMON, DESCEND, a reverb, Abyssdelay's delay and Hate Tape.
