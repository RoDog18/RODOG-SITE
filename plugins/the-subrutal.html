Features

Two paths

A DIRTY path and a CLEAN path run at once, and the CLEAN to DIRTY slider blends them.

Eight dirty heads

TOMB VT, B-7 GRAVE, OMEGA ROT, ROT ORANGE, PORTA-COFFIN, SUB-WAKE, DEAD SUNN T and SUBRUTAL.

Full chain

Sub synth, graphic EQ, drive, cab and IR with DI blend, mud tamer, comp, chorus, delay, reverb and tape.

A kick ducker that listens to the side chain or MIDI notes.
