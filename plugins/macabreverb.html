Features

Nine spaces

ROOM, PLATE, CRYPT, SPECTRE, REVERSE, COFFIN, CATHEDRAL, ASYLUM and SÉANCE.

Freeze and exorcise

FREEZE holds the tail forever. Hold EXORCISE and the tail is choked at once.

Tempo synced

LENGTH from 1/16 to 4 bars and PRE-DELAY from 1/128 to 1/8, dotted and triplet, or free in ms.

Advanced modules

CHAMBER, TAIL EQ, MOTION, SHIMMER, HAUNT, GATE and DUCK, all off until you want them.

MatchMe on the tail

Match the reverb to the dry signal, the side chain or a file. The dry is never touched.
