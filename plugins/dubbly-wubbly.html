Features

Six modes

TIGHT, WIDE, LEGION (eight players), TRACKS (a synthetic second take), QUAD and WUBBLY (players that sway).

Humanize

From robot-tight to sloppy, with DETUNE and WOBBLE.

Low end stays mono

Nothing below the split is doubled, so bass and kick stay solid.

Advanced modules

Octave ghosts, tone, Haas offset, ducking, spin, drift and a mono check with a correlation meter.
