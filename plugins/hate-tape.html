EVIL-HateTape-Tape

Tape and cassette machines, from 2-inch multitrack to VHS, with wear, wow and flutter, and tape stop.

PriceSet at launch

Installs through The Kennel. VST3 and AU, for macOS and Windows.

Walkthrough video coming soon

Features

Four machines

2" 16-track, 1/2" 2-track, cassette and VHS hi-fi, with five tape types and four speeds.

Calibrate it

BIAS, CAL and BASS, just like lining up a real machine.

HATE, WEAR and GENS

Drive the tape into pumping and fuzz, add the drift and dropouts of an old tape, and dub it up to 10 generations.

Transport

Wow and flutter, WARP for a stretched tape, and STOP for tape stops.

Head and noise

Azimuth, crosstalk, hiss and noise reduction that can be misaligned on purpose.
