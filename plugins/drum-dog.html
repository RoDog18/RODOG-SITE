Drum Dog

Everything a multitrack drum kit needs before the mix: level, align and trigger, on every drum track.

Price coming soon

Installs through the RoDog Product Manager. VST3 & AU, macOS & Windows.

▶Drum Dog walkthrough video coming soon

Features

LEVEL

Evens out every hit to a target, keeps ghost notes, and lets you drag any hit up or down by hand. Group snare top and bottom so they level together.

ALIGN

Lines every track up with a master track in time, polarity and phase, per band if you like. AUTO ALIGN works it out for you, or SLIDE it by ear.

TRIGGER

Turns hits into MIDI. Drag the notes onto a MIDI track, or send them live as the track plays. FETCH ignores bleed from the other drums.

The pack

Every Drum Dog in the session knows about the others. CAPTURE arms them all at once, and in ARA hosts (Logic, REAPER, Cubase, Studio One, Pro Tools) it reads the clips without playing.
