Features

Four bands

LOW, LOW MID, HIGH MID and HIGH, split with crossovers that add back flat. Every band has its own flavour, drive, mix, gain, solo, mute and power switch.

Five flavours

FORMALDEHYDE (tube), ROT (tape), GRAVE DIRT (fuzz), BONE SAW (rectify and fold) and CREMATE (bit and rate crush).

GUITAR, DRUMS, VOCALS and MASTER voice the engine for the job without touching your band settings.
