Support

Need a hand with a RoDog Audio plug-in? Email rodogaudio@protonmail.com and include:

  • the plug-in and its version
  • your computer (Mac or Windows, and the system version)
  • your music software (DAW) and its version
  • what happened, and what you expected to happen

We usually reply within two working days (Sydney time).

Lost your licence key or download?

Your licence keys and downloads are in the receipt email from our store, Lemon Squeezy. You can also find them any time on Lemon Squeezy's "My Orders" page, using the email you bought with.
