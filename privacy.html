Privacy policy

RoDog Audio collects as little about you as it can. This page explains what we do collect, why, and who else sees it.

When you buy

Our online store and checkout are run by Lemon Squeezy, which is the seller of record for our products. Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email address, billing country, payment details and tax information to take your payment, work out tax and send your receipt and licence key. Your card details go to Lemon Squeezy and its payment processor, never to us. Lemon Squeezy shares your name, email, country, what you bought and your licence key with us so we can support you. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy explains how they handle your data.

When you use our software

When The Kennel installs or updates plug-ins, or a plug-in checks its licence, your computer sends your licence key and a computer identifier so we can confirm the licence and count activations. We don't collect your audio, your projects or what you do in your music software.

When you visit this website

This website doesn't use cookies, ads or tracking. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those services see your IP address when you visit, as with any website.

When you email us

We keep your messages so we can help you and keep a record of support.

What we do with it

  • Deliver and support what you bought, and check licences.
  • Email you about your purchases and important product updates.
  • Send news or offers only if you've signed up for them. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We never sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing rodogaudio@protonmail.com. If you live in the EU or UK you also have rights under the GDPR. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
