Refund policy

We want you to be happy with every RoDog Audio plug-in.

If it doesn't work on your computer

If a plug-in won't install or run properly on a system we list as supported, email rodogaudio@protonmail.com within 14 days of buying it. We'll try to fix it with you first. If we can't, we'll give you a full refund.

Changed your mind

Because plug-ins are digital downloads, we don't normally refund a change of mind. If you bought the wrong plug-in by mistake and haven't used it, email us within 14 days and we'll sort it out.

How refunds work

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, through our store, Lemon Squeezy. When a purchase is refunded, its licence key stops working.

Your rights under Australian law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. If a product has a major failure, you're entitled to a refund or replacement. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.
